Sell in the first 4 days

Resale prices peak on opening weekend and then drop. Dune: Part Two buckets sold for an average of $64 in their first four days, even though listings were asking up to $846. List the same night you buy.

Take out fees and shipping

eBay keeps about 13% of the sale price, and shipping a bulky bucket usually costs $15–25. The estimated profit figures above already subtract both.

Avoid buckets sold everywhere

A bucket sold at every chain and online stays in supply. Exclusives from one chain, sold only in theaters, hold their value best. Tins usually resell for less than sculpted 3D buckets.

No Cinemark on Long Island

None of the Cinemark theaters I found are within driving distance. To get a Cinemark bucket, buy it as a ticket bundle from the Fandango Fan Store, or pick one of the AMC or Regal versions instead.

Go the day before opening

AMC and Regal usually start selling buckets on the Thursday preview night, the day before the Friday opening. The most popular ones sell out that night.

Showtimes

Preview times are posted a few weeks ahead of opening. The "Showtimes" links search for the latest times at that exact theater. Evening previews usually start between 6 and 7 PM.
